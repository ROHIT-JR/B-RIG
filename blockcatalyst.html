BLOCK #001 CONFIRMED the idea

Why we’re doing this

Most of us learned blockchain from slides. You can pass every exam on consensus and hashing and still have never shipped something that a stranger could break.

So: one room, one continuous day, and an idea you committed to in advance. You arrive with a plan. You leave with a prototype that runs, a repository, and the experience of explaining your own design decisions to people who do this for a living.

Some of what gets built here will be rough. That’s fine. Rough and working beats polished and theoretical.

    BLOCK #003 CONFIRMED who can come

    Can you enter?

    Almost certainly yes, if you are a student and you can find three others.

    • Undergraduate or postgraduate — both welcome, and you can mix them in one team
    • Exactly four of you
    • All four from the same college
    • Different departments on one team is encouraged, not just allowed
    • Bring your institute ID to the venue

    You do not need to have shipped a smart contract before. A good chunk of the people in that room will be doing this for the first time, and there will be mentors walking around all night.

    We would especially like to see more women’s teams this year. There’s a dedicated award for the best all-women team, and it is not a consolation prize.

      BLOCK #005 CONFIRMED who we are

      The people running this

      B-RIG is a student group at TIFAC-CORE in Cyber Security, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore. We spend our time on blockchain forensics, OSINT, decentralised applications, smart contract security and the metaverse — mostly by building things and arguing about them afterwards.

      We ran the first BlockCatalyst in 2023. This one is bigger, open to other colleges, and organised by students who have been on your side of the table recently enough to remember what a badly-run hackathon feels like.

      If you are deciding whether it is worth the travel: message us on Instagram and ask. A person will reply.
