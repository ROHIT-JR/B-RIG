What we work on

Five things, mostly

Forensics

Tracing transactions, reading wallet behaviour, and working out what actually happened on a chain after the fact.

OSINT

Open-source intelligence pointed at on-chain data — provenance, attribution, and the slow work of investigation.

DApps

Decentralised applications that solve something real, for people who are not crypto traders.

Smart contract security

Contract vulnerabilities, protocol risk, access control. Breaking things carefully, then fixing them.

NFTs and metaverse

Identity, ownership and trust inside immersive environments, where the token has to earn its place.

How that happens

Workshops, projects, and one long night a year

We run sessions and training through the year for members, take on collaborative projects, and organise events open to the wider campus. New members get trained across all five areas rather than being dropped into one.

  • Around thirty new members join each year
  • Training across forensics, OSINT, DApps, contract security and the metaverse
  • A certificate on completing the programme
  • Projects you can actually point at afterwards
  • Alumni stay on the rolls once they have finished

If you are a student here and any of this sounds like your kind of thing, message us. Recruitment opens once a year and we would rather you asked early than found out late.
